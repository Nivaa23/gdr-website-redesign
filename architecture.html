PRACTICE & CAPABILITIES

Engineering-Driven Architecture for Civic and Institutional Built Environments

Geo Designs & Research provides holistic architectural and master planning consultancy across institutional, healthcare, civic administration, and large-scale residential infrastructure. Combining multi-disciplinary engineering depth with functional spatial planning, our team creates enduring environments tailored to site geography, urban context, and statutory guidelines.

From concept ideation and detailed schematic design through structural coordination, NBC statutory compliance, and tender documentation, GDR ensures architectural excellence is seamlessly integrated with structural integrity and sustainable building services.
