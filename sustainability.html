ENVIRONMENTALLY SUSTAINABLE SOLUTIONS

ENVIRONMENTAL MEASUREMENT & TESTING

FIELD MONITORING AND ANALYSIS

Geo Designs & Research delivers comprehensive environmental field monitoring, sampling, physical testing, and laboratory chemical-biological analysis in support of large-scale infrastructure, institutional developments, and industrial projects. Our environmental engineering protocols adhere rigorously to statutory parameters established by MoEFCC, CPCB, and State Pollution Control Boards.

Water Quality

Water Quality, Biological & Chemical Analysis, Microbiological & Visual Analysis

In-depth physicochemical testing, heavy metal concentration quantification, dissolved solids assessment, and microbiological screening for surface, ground, and industrial effluent discharge compliance.

Air Quality Sampling

Air Quality and Physical Sampling, Analysis and Other Testing

Source emission monitoring, isokinetic stack sampling, particulate sizing (PM10 & PM2.5), flue gas profiling, and gaseous pollutant determination (SOx, NOx, CO, VOCs) under operational factory loads.

Acoustics & Vibration

Sound Emission/Vibration Monitoring, Noise Measurement, Air Quality and Related Monitoring

High-precision day/night Leq noise level logging, octave-band acoustic profiling, structural vibration velocity recording, and impact boundary mapping for sensitive receptor corridors.

Soil & Material

Soil Analysis, Physico-Chemical Parameters and Material Analysis

Comprehensive soil texture testing, electrical conductivity, heavy metal leaching, organic carbon index, cation exchange capacity, and subsurface fertility evaluation for reclamation planning.

Ambient & Meteorology

Ambient Air Quality Monitoring, Meteorological Analysis and Related Testing

Automated continuous and baseline ambient air monitoring integrated with on-site micro-meteorological stations measuring wind speed, direction vectors, humidity, solar radiation, and rainfall data.

SPECIALIZED CAPABILITIES

Environmental Management Services

Structured engineering advisory covering remediation, statutory environmental clearance, resource conservation, and long-term ecological stewardship across civil infrastructure and industrial assets.

SERVICE 01

Reclamation and Recovery System

Engineering design and technical facilitation of land reclamation schemes, brownfield remediation, industrial byproduct recovery circuits, and secondary resource harvesting systems. We devise site-specific engineering methods to decontaminate degraded land parcels, stabilize mine overburden, and reclaim valuable materials from industrial process waste streams.

SERVICE 02

Solid and Hazardous Waste Management

Development of integrated municipal solid waste (MSW) plans, secured landfill engineering designs, hazardous waste classification, containment facility modeling, and circular waste handling procedures. Our team establishes cradle-to-grave containment systems adhering strictly to Hazardous and Other Wastes Rules, ensuring safe disposal and resource recovery.

SERVICE 03

Environmental Management Systems

Formulation and deployment of structured Environmental Management Systems (EMS) aligned with ISO 14001:2015 frameworks. We assist public sector agencies and industrial plants in institutionalizing standard operating environmental procedures, pollution prevention audits, operational key performance indicators, and emergency environmental response protocols.

SERVICE 04

Green Certifications (Buildings/Factories)

Comprehensive advisory and facilitation for prestigious green building certification ratings including GRIHA, LEED, and IGBC standards for commercial campuses, educational facilities, and industrial factories. Our engineering scope spans building envelope optimization, daylight simulations, embodied carbon reductions, and sustainable site selection.

SERVICE 05

Stormwater and Water Drainage Systems

Hydraulic design of sustainable urban drainage systems (SuDS), regional stormwater conveyance channels, retention reservoirs, and groundwater percolation networks. Utilizing hydrodynamic modeling tools, we size drainage infrastructure to prevent urban inundation while recharging natural subterranean aquifers.

SERVICE 06

Climate Change and Sustainability

Climate vulnerability risk profiling, greenhouse gas (GHG) inventory compilation, climate resilience master planning, and decarbonization roadmap design for urban development and highway networks. We help infrastructure developers adapt to shifting precipitation regimes, temperature extremes, and prolonged dry spells.

SERVICE 07

Water Audits

Rigorous end-to-end water balance audits for industrial plants, municipal utility networks, and institutional complexes. Our engineers pinpoint transmission losses, assess fixture efficiencies, quantify recycling potential, and prepare technical feasibility reports for industrial wastewater recycling and rainwater harvesting systems.

SERVICE 08

Environmental Compliance

Advisory and technical representation for obtaining Consent to Establish (CTE) and Consent to Operate (CTO) from State Pollution Control Boards. We manage mandatory post-clearance compliance tracking, periodic statutory environmental audits, hazardous waste return filings, and continuous online monitoring system (OCEMS) verification.

SERVICE 09

Environmental Impact Assessment and CRZ Clearances

End-to-end Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) baseline studies, predictive modeling, Environmental Management Plan (EMP) formulation, public consultation support, and Coastal Regulation Zone (CRZ) clearance documentation for ports, maritime infrastructure, bridges, and coastal highways under MoEFCC guidelines.

ESG SOLUTIONS

Integrating ESG Principles Across Engineering Lifecycles

Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) considerations are no longer separate compliance exercises; they are central to sustainable business performance, bankable infrastructure financing, and responsible development. Geo Designs & Research provides strategic and technical advisory enabling corporations and public infrastructure authorities to evaluate ecological impacts, uphold worker and community well-being, and maintain rigorous corporate transparency.

STRATEGIC FRAMEWORKS

HOLISTIC SUSTAINABLE BUSINESS STRATEGY IMBIBING

A structured suite of strategic advisory services designed to embed sustainability principles directly into corporate decision-making, investment evaluations, and operational engineering practices.

1. ESG Environmental and Reporting

Preparation of comprehensive corporate sustainability reports aligned with recognized global frameworks (GRI, BRSR, TCFD), documenting resource efficiency, carbon metrics, and environmental performance.

2. ESG Compliance

Continuous monitoring and legal compliance assessments against evolving environmental legislation, corporate disclosure regulations, labor safety codes, and institutional lending covenants.

3. Sustainability Assessment

Comprehensive baseline audits across energy, water, materials, and emissions lifecycles to quantify environmental footprints and establish realistic improvement trajectories for industrial operations.

4. ESG Valuation

Quantification of environmental risks and intangible sustainability assets during mergers, acquisitions, infrastructure transactions, and project financing evaluations.

5. Decarbonization

Engineering roadmaps for transitioning energy systems toward renewables, optimizing thermal efficiency, reducing fugitive greenhouse gas emissions, and adopting low-carbon materials.

6. Waste Management

Implementation of closed-loop industrial ecology models, waste-to-resource recovery programs, hazardous waste minimization, and zero-landfill diversion protocols.

7. Eco Due Diligence

Phase I and Phase II Environmental Site Assessments (ESA) assessing legacy contamination liabilities, groundwater pollution, ecological zoning constraints, and regulatory exposure.

8. Impact Measurement & Social Responsibility

Methodological frameworks for evaluating socioeconomic benefits, community stakeholder engagement, resettlement advisory, and corporate social investment efficacy.

CIRCULAR ENGINEERING PARADIGM

NET ZERO, ZERO WASTE OR ZERO LIQUID DISCHARGE

Achieving deep environmental sustainability requires moving beyond incremental abatement toward systemic circularity. Geo Designs & Research develops robust engineering architectures enabling industrial complexes, municipal authorities, and infrastructure hubs to implement Net Zero, Zero Waste, and Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD) systems.

PILLAR 01

Net Zero Carbon

Minimizing direct Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions through high-efficiency building designs, on-site solar PV installations, industrial heat recovery, and strategic transition to electrified processes.

PILLAR 02

Zero Waste to Landfill

Comprehensive waste segregation, organic compost conversion, secondary scrap recovery, and coprocessing of non-recyclable fractions in cement kilns to eliminate reliance on open landfills.

PILLAR 03

Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD)

Advanced wastewater recycling combining membrane bioreactors (MBR), reverse osmosis (RO), and thermal mechanical vapor recompression (MVR) evaporators to recover 95%+ of effluent as pure condensate.
