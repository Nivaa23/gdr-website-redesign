GROUND GEODETICS & TOPOGRAPHY

FIELD SURVEY

Precision ground surveying providing authoritative spatial baselines for highway corridors, railway alignments, canal networks, and industrial complexes.

Our field survey teams deploy high-precision Electronic Total Stations (ETS) and dual-frequency Differential GPS (DGPS) systems to establish primary geodetic control networks tied to universal WGS-84/UTM datums. From longitudinal and cross-sectional profiling to cadastral revenue boundary demarcation, we deliver millimeter-accurate geospatial data complying with Survey of India, Indian Railways, and MoRTH specifications.

  • • Electronic Total Station (ETS) Topography
  • • DGPS Geodetic Control Networks
  • • Longitudinal (L-Sec) & Cross-Sections (X-Sec)
  • • Cadastral Boundary & Revenue Land Demarcation
01 // TOPOGRAPHY

Contour & Surface Mapping

High-density digital ground elevation models and micro-contouring for industrial plant layouts, drainage design, and township master plans.

02 // ALIGNMENT

Corridor Final Location Surveys

End-to-end Final Location Surveys (FLS), horizontal and vertical curvature optimization, and Right of Way (ROW) marking for roads and railways.

03 // GEODETICS

Permanent Benchmark Monumentation

Establishment of robust concrete geodetic pillars with verified GTS benchmark ties to ensure continuous horizontal and vertical control throughout construction.

HIGH-SPEED CORRIDOR DIGITIZATION

MOBILE LIDAR SURVEY

Vehicle-mounted 360-degree laser scanning systems capturing millions of 3D spatial points per second at highway operating speeds without traffic disruption.

Our Mobile LiDAR capabilities deliver dense, calibrated point cloud models integrated with tactical-grade Inertial Measurement Units (IMU) and RTK-GNSS receivers. This enables sub-centimeter pavement condition surveys, structural clearance verification for bridges and overhead utilities, road asset inventories, and comprehensive corridor Digital Terrain Models (DTM).

  • • Acquisition Rate: Up to 1.2M points/second
  • • Pavement & Overhead Clearance Auditing
  • • Highway Right-of-Way (ROW) Asset Inventory
  • • Calibrated 360° Spherical Imagery Overlay
01 // POINT CLOUDS

Corridor 3D Digitization

Comprehensive point cloud datasets allowing civil engineers to extract lane widths, median profiles, edge of pavements, and road furniture with survey accuracy.

02 // ASSET INVENTORY

Automated Road Furniture Extraction

Extraction and GIS cataloging of road signs, guardrails, culverts, streetlights, and drainage assets for highway concessionaires and NHAI compliance.

03 // SAFETY & SPEED

Zero Traffic Disruption

High-speed corridor survey without hazardous road closures or field personnel exposure on active multi-lane expressways.

UAV PHOTOGRAMMETRY & AERIAL GEOSPATIAL

DRONE SURVEY

Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) photogrammetry and LiDAR surveying providing rapid, cost-effective spatial models across vast, inaccessible terrain.

Utilizing commercial survey-grade drones equipped with high-resolution full-frame sensors and RTK/PPK positioning, we generate millimeter-scale orthomosaic maps, Digital Surface Models (DSM), and Digital Terrain Models (DTM). Drone surveys are vital for monitoring large-scale earthwork volumetrics, mining stockyards, watershed reservoirs, and multi-kilometer pipeline corridors.

  • • High-Resolution True Orthomosaic Imagery
  • • Digital Elevation & Terrain Models (DEM / DTM)
  • • Cut-and-Fill Volumetric Stockyard Calculations
  • • Ground Control Points (GCP) RTK Verification
01 // PHOTOGRAMMETRY

Orthomosaics & GIS Integration

Georeferenced spatial orthomosaics with sub-2cm ground sampling distance (GSD), seamlessly integrable into ArcGIS, QGIS, and CAD platforms.

02 // VOLUMETRICS

Earthwork & Stockpile Quantification

Precise 3D mesh volume computation for dam embankments, highway cuttings, aggregate stockpiles, and quarry extraction auditing.

03 // RECONNAISSANCE

Inaccessible Terrain Mapping

Aerial mapping of dense ravines, steep hillsides, waterlogged floodplains, and remote transmission corridors inaccessible to ground survey crews.
