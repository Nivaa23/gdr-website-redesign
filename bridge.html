Representative Bridge Projects

Major River Bridge & Roadway Infrastructure

CLIENT
IRCON International • Ministry of Railways
SCOPE OF SERVICES

Comprehensive structural analysis, detailed engineering design, hydraulic investigations, and Detailed Project Report (DPR) preparation complying with IRC and MoRTH standards for multi-span river crossings.

River Bridges & Flyovers Detailed Project Reports (DPR) IRC & MoRTH Compliance
PROJECT 02 // FREIGHT CORRIDOR PUBLIC SECTOR ENTERPRISE

Railway Over Bridge (ROB) & Grade Separators

CLIENT
Dedicated Freight Corridor Corporation of India (DFCCIL)
SCOPE OF SERVICES

Complete structural modeling, composite steel-concrete girder design, deep foundation detailing, substructure analysis, and track-clearance verification across active railway electrified corridors.

Railway Over Bridges (ROB) Grade Separators Composite Girder Design
PROJECT 03 // URBAN MOBILITY URBAN DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY

Urban Flyover & Multi-Level Elevated Interchanges

CLIENT
Ahmedabad Urban Development Authority (AUDA)
SCOPE OF SERVICES

Multi-level interchange structural layout, traffic circulation integration, precast prestressed concrete girder detailing, pier cap optimization, and construction staging methodology planning.

Elevated Flyovers Multi-Level Interchanges Prestressed Concrete
PROJECT 04 // DIAGNOSTICS & RETROFITTING MUNICIPAL CORPORATION

Bridge Health Assessment & Structural Rehabilitation

CLIENT
Surat Municipal Corporation (SMC)
SCOPE OF SERVICES

Bridge Health & Structural Audits NDT Field Investigations Rehabilitation & Retrofitting
