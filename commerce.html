CAPABILITIES • SECTION 03

Services Provided

SERVICE 01 Estimation

Approximate Estimate

An approximate estimate is a preliminary calculation of the quantities and approximate values of materials and work expected during a project. How closely the estimated cost matches the actual cost depends on the accuracy of the estimating methods and the correct evaluation of the work.

SERVICE 02 Estimation

Detailed Estimate

A detailed estimate of project cost is prepared by listing and determining the quantities and costs of everything a contractor is required to provide and execute for the construction or completion of the project.

SERVICE 03 Analysis

Market Analysis

Market analysis evaluates market trends, product mix, customer needs, and effective marketing strategies. It helps clients make informed business decisions and understand the construction industry's value chain.

SERVICE 04 Analysis

Rate Analysis

Rate analysis determines the cost of each construction work item by quantifying the required materials, labour, machinery, contractor's profit, and other miscellaneous expenses needed to complete the work.

SERVICE 05 Tendering

Draft Tender Paper

A draft tender paper contains relevant details, including qualification requirements and the selection process, to guide technically qualified bidders through a comparative evaluation of offers based on a specified scientific or systematic approach.

SERVICE 06 Reporting

Detailed Project Report

A detailed project report is a final, detailed proposal for a project. It describes the project's scope, objectives, basic requirements, and other relevant information to support project evaluation and implementation.
