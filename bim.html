DISCIPLINE 02 // CIVIL & ARCHITECTURAL BIM

BIM

BUILDING INFORMATION MODELLING

Constructible Models for Better Construction

Multi-Disciplinary: Architecture • Structure • MEP / Dimensions: 3D Modelling to 7D Lifecycle / Standards: ISO 9001:2015 • EMS ISO 14001:2015
COORDINATED MULTI-DISCIPLINARY BIM MODEL
OVERVIEW • SECTION 02

What is BIM

Multi-disciplinary 3D to 7D Building Information Modelling, automated clash detection, quantity takeoff, construction simulation (4D/5D), and BIM model auditing for complex civil, institutional, and commercial infrastructure.

  • Constructible Digital Twin: High-precision parametric geometry integrating architectural envelopes, structural frameworks, and MEP engineering networks.
  • Single Source of Truth: Coordinated project intelligence ensuring zero discrepancy between 2D shop drawings, 3D spatial models, and Bill of Quantities (BoQ).
VALUE PROPOSITION • SECTION 03

Why BIM?

Building Information Modeling (BIM) creates coordinated 3D model using database for a construction project. BIM Model offers designers and owners complete control over the design. BIM Model is very useful for effective interaction between Consultants / Designers, Engineers, Contractors and Sub-Contractors. This helps them take quick decision, prepare accurate construction documents, improve constructability, reduce documentation errors and operational issues and ensure better construction management.

01 // CONSTRUCTIBILITY

Clash Mitigation

Pre-construction spatial conflict detection between architectural, structural, and MEP trades to eradicate costly on-site rework.

02 // PRECISION

Automated Quantities

Model-driven Bill of Quantities (BoQ) and Bar Bending Schedules (BBS) for transparent cost control and tendering confidence.

03 // SIMULATION

4D / 5D Timeline

Synchronized construction sequence simulation and cash flow forecasting to safeguard project commissioning milestones.

04 // COLLABORATION

Lifecycle Governance

Coordinated multi-party review sessions and cloud-based issue tracking providing unified oversight from design to facility handover.

GOVERNANCE • SECTION 07

Quality Process & Hold Points

ISO 9001:2015 QUALITY MANAGEMENT SYSTEM EMS ISO 14001:2015 ENVIRONMENTAL MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

QC process is ISO 9001:2015 managed by qualified QC team. We have implemented Environmental Management System EMS ISO 14001:2015.

The main objective of the Quality Control (QC) process is to deliver errors and rework by ensuring client satisfaction and our detailed QC team is well equipped with a highly qualified and experienced Manager.

HOLD POINT 01

QC1

Initial architectural & structural model integrity verification, parametric bounding check, and standards compliance review.

HOLD POINT 02

QC2

Interdisciplinary clash detection (Arch vs Struct vs MEPF), tolerance audit, clearance verification, and resolution reporting.

HOLD POINT 03

QC1

Secondary verification checkpoint, 2D sheet extraction check, metadata audit, and final model validation prior to client release.

HOLD POINT 04 // FINAL

DELIVERY TO CLIENT

Formal delivery of coordinated constructible 3D–7D models, shop drawings, BoQ quantity extractions, and handover sign-off.
