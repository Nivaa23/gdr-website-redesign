ENGINEERING OVERVIEW

The Role of Detailed Project Reports (DPR)

A Detailed Project Report (DPR) serves as the definitive engineering and techno-economic blueprint for statutory project approval, capital budget appropriation, environmental clearance, and on-ground execution.

Geo Designs & Research integrates multidisciplinary engineering expertise to formulate comprehensive DPRs, synchronizing hydraulic watershed dynamics with road geometry, pavement durability, and environmental compliance.

  • Alignment Surveys & Route Studies: Topographical profiling, contour mapping, and digital corridor modeling.
  • Hydrological & Hydraulic Modeling: Catchment runoff simulations, HFL computations, and scour depth analysis.
  • Pavement & Traffic Engineering: Classified volume counts, axle load surveys, and flexible/rigid structural designs.
  • Comprehensive DPR Documentation: Bill of quantities, cost estimation, economic IRR, and statutory clearance dockets.

CORRIDOR INFRASTRUCTURE

Highway DPR Overview

Highway Detailed Project Reports (DPR) provide the complete technical, operational, and financial framework required to deliver robust arterial corridors, expressways, and bypass routes adhering to IRC (Indian Roads Congress) and MoRTH standards.

The scope encompasses multi-lane highway alignment, intersection geometry, subgrade soil stabilization, traffic growth projections, pavement structural design, safety audits, and life-cycle cost analysis.

Geometric Alignment & Right-of-Way (ROW) Horizontal and vertical profile optimization, curve transitions, and sight-distance verification.
Pavement Structure & Axle Load Engineering Mechanistic-empirical design for flexible asphalt and rigid concrete pavements based on IRC:37 and IRC:58.
Drainage & Cross-Structure Integration Longitudinal median drains, side drains, chute drains, and cross-drainage culverts designed to prevent subgrade saturation.
