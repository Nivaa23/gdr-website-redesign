THREE DECADES OF ENGINEERING EXCELLENCE & NATION BUILDING

From our founding as an advanced material testing laboratory in 1991 to becoming one of India's premier multidisciplinary engineering consultancies.

1991

Founding of Geo Test House

2000

Vadodara Lab & Bhuj Branch

Expansion of central testing facilities to 1,500 sq.ft and establishment of the Bhuj regional branch facility to support critical post-earthquake reconstruction.

PHASE 02 DISASTER RELIEF
2005

Dedicated 8,000 Sq.Ft Campus

Relocation to a dedicated 8,000 sq.ft specialized testing and geotechnical engineering campus in Vadodara, adding metallurgical and environmental testing.

PHASE 03 INFRASTRUCTURE
2007

Incorporation of GDR Pvt. Ltd.

Incorporation of Geo Designs & Research Pvt. Ltd., rapidly expanding operations into Highway DPR route engineering, Bridge Design, and PMC Supervisions.

PHASE 04 CORPORATE ENTITY
2009

International Expansion

Established Metcon Testing Laboratory in Dubai to deliver high-precision testing across the Middle East.

PHASE 05 GLOBAL REACH
2010

Accreditations & Regional Growth

Achieved NABL accreditation under ISO/IEC 17025 and ISO 9001 certification. Expanded with a branch and laboratory in Bharuch.

PHASE 06 ACCREDITATION
2012

Expansion of Laboratory Services

Undertook laboratory consultancy in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia and expanded with branches and laboratories in Gandhinagar and Junagadh.

PHASE 07 REGIONAL HUBS

International Project Expertise

PHASE 08 HIGH-RISE RIGOR
2014

Further Regional Expansion

Expanded branch operations to Navsari, Anand and Godhra, establishing immediate on-site mobilization.

PHASE 09 WESTERN NETWORK

Diversification of Engineering Services

Expanded facilities from 8,000 to 16,000 sq.ft. Added environmental testing, non-destructive testing (NDT), architectural design, and quantity surveying.

PHASE 10 16K SQ.FT EXPANSION
2017

Infrastructure Expansion

Expanded facilities from 16,000 sq.ft to 24,000 sq.ft and accelerated large-scale bridge, highway, and corridor mandates across Western India with dedicated PMC teams.

PHASE 11 CORRIDORS

Merger & Testing Milestone

PHASE 12 457 NABL TESTS
2020

Advanced Engineering Capabilities

Expanded into MEPF, green building, and Building Information Modeling (BIM), alongside the introduction of Falling Weight Deflectometer (FWD) and Network Survey Vehicle (NSV) capabilities.

PHASE 13 BIM • FWD • NSV

Training & Continued Growth
