TESTING CAPABILITIES

Specialized Testing Laboratories

Comprehensive multi-disciplinary testing scopes organized into dedicated, certified laboratory divisions.

LABORATORY 01

High Tech Material & Concrete Lab

Physical and compressive verification of hydraulic binders, cement, coarse/fine aggregates, concrete mix designs, and precast products.

  • Compressive strength of concrete cubes, cylinders & core specimens (IS 516)
  • High-performance concrete mix design from M10 to M80 (IS 10262)
  • Cement fineness, standard consistency, setting times & soundness (IS 4031)
  • Aggregate sieve analysis, flakiness, elongation & crushing value (IS 2386)
  • Precast concrete pavers, solid/hollow blocks & kerb stones testing
LABORATORY 02

Soil Testing & Geotechnical Studies

Engineering classification, shear strength, permeability, consolidation, and bearing capacity determinations for foundation safety.

  • Grain size distribution, sieve analysis & hydrometer sedimentation (IS 2720-4)
  • Liquid limit, plastic limit & plasticity index determinations (IS 2720-5)
  • Standard & Modified Proctor compaction moisture-density tests (IS 2720-7/8)
  • California Bearing Ratio (CBR) soaked & unsoaked for pavement subgrades
  • Direct shear, triaxial compression & unconfined compressive strength (UCS)
LABORATORY 03

Metallurgical & Chemical Lab

Mechanical tension, bend, chemical elemental composition, and durability analysis of structural steel, rebar, and construction chemicals.

  • Tensile strength, 0.2% proof stress & percentage elongation of TMT rebar (IS 1786)
  • Cold bend and re-bend testing for reinforcement bars & structural couplers
  • Chemical analysis for Carbon, Sulfur, Phosphorus content in steel alloys
  • Chemical testing of water for construction suitability (IS 456 & IS 3025)
  • Chloride, sulfate, pH value, and organic matter quantification in soil & water
LABORATORY 04

Highway Material Testing

Pavement engineering tests for paving bitumen, modified bitumen, asphalt mixes, and road base aggregates adhering to MoRTH and IRC standards.

  • Bitumen penetration, softening point, ductility & elastic recovery (IS 1201-1220)
  • Kinematic and absolute viscosity analysis for VG-10, VG-30 & VG-40 grades
  • Marshall stability, flow value & volumetric properties for DBM and BC mixes
  • Bitumen extraction and binder content analysis using automated centrifuges
  • Stripping value, water sensitivity, and aggregate Los Angeles abrasion testing
LABORATORY 05

Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) Lab

In-situ diagnostic evaluation of hardened concrete structures, bridges, heritage monuments, and industrial foundations without structural damage.

  • Ultrasonic Pulse Velocity (UPV) test for concrete quality & crack depth (IS 13311-1)
  • Digital Rebound Hammer (Schmidt Hammer) for surface compressive estimation (IS 13311-2)
  • Diamond core drilling, specimen trimming & laboratory compression testing (IS 516)
  • Electromagnetic cover meter & Profometer scanning for rebar location and cover
  • Half-cell potential corrosion survey for reinforcement durability assessment
LABORATORY 06

Environmental Monitoring & Analysis

Environmental baseline monitoring, ambient air quality analysis, noise mapping, and industrial effluent characterization for regulatory clearance.

  • Ambient air quality monitoring for PM10, PM2.5, SO2, NOx & Carbon Monoxide
  • Workplace and community environmental noise level surveys using Type 1 sound meters
  • Potable water and industrial wastewater / effluent physical & chemical testing
  • Soil fertility, salinity, electrical conductivity, and heavy metal screening
  • Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) baseline sampling & compliance testing
